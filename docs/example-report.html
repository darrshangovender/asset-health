Masked values (out of range, stuck, spikes) are removed before any detector sees them; an asset whose data cannot support a decision is blocked rather than scored.

Method

Per-asset healthy baselines are learned on a commissioning window; every channel is compared with its regime-expected value (load, speed; temperature as rise above ambient), so a busy shift is not a fault. CUSUM, an Isolation Forest and an explainable rule layer are fused; an alert opens after a debounce and closes with hysteresis. RUL combines health-index trend extrapolation with quantile regression and a conformal margin fitted on separate seeds. Costs behind the alert thresholds are illustrative.
